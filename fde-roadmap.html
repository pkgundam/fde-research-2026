Research report

What Forward Deployed Engineer roles actually demand

A skills map built from 305 live FDE job postings across 142 companies, extracted against a fixed taxonomy and split by whether a skill is the job or merely a requirement.

1 · What an FDE does

The loop every posting describes, in one form or another.

  1. DiscoverSit with the customer; find the workflow that actually hurts.
  2. ScopeTurn the pain into a bounded, measurable first deliverable.
  3. PrototypeShip something rough in days, on their data.
  4. IntegrateWire it into the systems they already run.
  5. DeployGet it into their environment, under their constraints.
  6. EvaluateMeasure against the outcome you scoped, not vibes.
  7. ImproveClose the gaps the eval exposed.
  8. Feed backBring what the field taught you to the product team.

3 · Frequency vs criticality

Frequency (x): how often a skill appears. Criticality (y): the share of its mentions that sit in a responsibilities section rather than requirements or nice-to-haves — i.e. how often the skill is the job. Faded points have fewer than 5 mentions.

Top-left — Hidden core: rarely listed, but when listed it is the work. Top-right — The job: common and central. Learn these first. Bottom-left — Peripheral: occasional nice-to-haves. Bottom-right — Table stakes: everyone asks; nobody hires for it alone.

Extraction. Each posting was segmented into responsibilities / requirements / nice-to-have sections (224 by explicit headers, 81 inferred) and passed once, with the full closed taxonomy, to claude-sonnet-5 using a fixed prompt. Every returned skill was validated against the taxonomy; 0 out-of-vocabulary mentions were logged and reviewed. Adzuna: not used. LinkedIn and Indeed were not scraped.
